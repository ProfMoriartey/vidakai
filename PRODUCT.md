# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Owners of small and mid-sized local businesses (restaurants, cafes, coffee shops, clinics, schools, fabric and automotive companies, small digital companies) who need a website and are deciding who to trust to build it. They arrive from a referral, a search, or a link in a message, usually on a phone, often between other work. They are not developers. They judge by proof (real sites already live), a clear offer, and how direct the studio sounds.

## Product Purpose

Vidakai Studio designs and builds websites and web apps for businesses: either starting from a ready-made template for their kind of business, or as a custom build. The site exists to get a business owner to start a conversation (contact form, email or WhatsApp). Success is a qualified enquiry.

## Positioning

Design, build, hosting and launch are handled in one place, with one point of contact from brief to launch. Ready-made templates per business type make the start fast. Real shipped work for real local businesses (Menupedia, Winbrella Fabrics, Hayat Goz Clinic, Tuzemen Group, Hangflow, Hastory, a QR code generator) is the proof. Arabic and other right-to-left languages are handled properly, not bolted on.

## Brand commitments

- Name: "Vidakai Studio".
- Voice: neutral studio voice. No "I", "we" or "our team". Don't mention or stress that one person runs the studio, and don't name the owner on the site. Never claim a team, offices, an agency of many, or a client count.
- Direct and plain: say what is built, for whom, and how long it takes. No hype.
- Dark only, no light theme (owner decision, 2026-11-22).
- English only for now; layout stays RTL-safe so Arabic can be added later (owner decision, 2026-11-22).
- No invented results, testimonials, metrics or prices. Case study copy comes from the owner.

## Accessibility

WCAG AA contrast, keyboard reachable, respects prefers-reduced-motion, works in RTL (Arabic).

## Open decisions

None at the moment. Case-study copy, contact details, domain and timelines are settled (2026-11-23).
