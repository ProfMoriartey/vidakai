# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Owners of small and mid-sized local businesses (restaurants, cafes, coffee shops, clinics, schools, fabric and automotive companies, small digital companies) who need a website and are deciding who to trust to build it. They arrive from a referral, a search, or a link in a message, usually on a phone, often between other work. They are not developers. They judge by proof (real sites already live), a clear offer, and how direct the person sounds.

## Product Purpose

Vidakai Studio is one developer who designs and builds websites and web apps for businesses: either starting from a ready-made template for their kind of business, or as a custom build. The site exists to get a business owner to start a conversation (contact form, email or WhatsApp). Success is a qualified enquiry.

## Positioning

The person who designs the site is the person who builds it and answers the messages. Ready-made templates per business type make the start fast. Real shipped work for real local businesses (Menupedia, Winbrella Fabrics, Hayat Goz Clinic, Tuzemen Group, Hangflow, Hastory, a QR code generator) is the proof. Arabic and other right-to-left languages are handled properly, not bolted on.

## Brand commitments

- Name: "Vidakai Studio".
- Solo developer. Never claim a team, "our team", an agency of many, offices, managed cloud infrastructure, international marketing, or a client count.
- Voice: direct, first person. Says what is built, for whom, and how long it takes. No hype.
- Dark only, no light theme (owner decision, 2026-11-22).
- English only for now; layout stays RTL-safe so Arabic can be added later (owner decision, 2026-11-22).
- No invented results, testimonials, metrics or prices. Case study copy comes from the owner.

## Accessibility

WCAG AA contrast, keyboard reachable, respects prefers-reduced-motion, works in RTL (Arabic).

## Open decisions

- Real case-study lines for all seven projects (owner to supply).
- Social links, WhatsApp number and contact email to show publicly.
- Production domain (code currently assumes https://vidakai.com).
- Timelines and "from" figures for What's included (owner to supply; no prices invented).
